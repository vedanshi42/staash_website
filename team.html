How we work at Staash.
Roles over titles

We are building our organisation around what people actually do. When you join Staash, your role is defined by your domain and your contribution — not where you sit on a hierarchy chart.

Ownership over instruction

Every person who works on Staash owns their domain. We expect you to understand the problem, make decisions, and tell us when the approach needs to change — not wait to be told what to do.

Proximity to the problem

We work closest to the stores and store owners we serve. That proximity is not a phase of the company — it is a permanent principle. The best decisions come from the shortest distance to the actual problem.
