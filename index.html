The Problem
Indian retail loses crores every single day.

Checkout queues push customers to abandon full carts and walk out. Shrinkage from theft and billing errors silently drains margins. The solutions that exist were built for Western supermarkets at Western budgets — they have never worked for India.

No Indian store owner should have to choose between serving customers well and protecting their inventory. Staash solves both at once.
