The Customer Experience
Every step designed to respect your customer’s time.

From the moment a customer opens Staash to the moment they walk out, every interaction is intentional, fast, and transparent.

01
Enter the store

The customer opens the Staash app and enters the store. The store is identified automatically. If they pre-listed items, their cart is already waiting.

Customer experience
  • Store confirmed instantly on app
  • Pre-listed cart loaded if set up
  • No registration queue or sign-in counter
02
Pick items and scan

As the customer picks items off the shelf, they scan them using their phone. Each item is added to their cart with price and running total visible at all times.

Customer experience
  • Item name and price confirmed per scan
  • Running total visible throughout
  • Items can be removed or adjusted freely
03
Shop freely

The customer browses at their own pace with no pressure to queue. The store’s intelligence layer monitors the floor quietly, accounting for every cart without interrupting anyone.

Store benefit
  • All active carts visible to staff
  • Staff free to assist rather than cashier
  • Anomalies surfaced automatically
04
Validate at checkout

When ready to leave, the customer shows a QR code from the app at the checkout point. Cart is confirmed in seconds. No cashier needed, no paper, no waiting.

What happens
  • Cart verified at checkout point
  • Total locked and payment triggered
  • Entire process takes under 30 seconds
05
Pay and walk out

Payment via UPI, card, or wallet. Digital receipt sent immediately. The customer exits with no queue, no paper slip, and no friction of any kind.

Customer receives
  • Instant digital receipt on app
  • Purchase history saved automatically
  • No printed bill, no separate counter
